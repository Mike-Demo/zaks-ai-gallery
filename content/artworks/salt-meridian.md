# Salt Meridian — artist notes (SAMPLE — replace with Zak's real notes)

## Inspiration

"A coastline remembered wrong, on purpose." Zak painted Salt Meridian
from a postcard of a place they'd never visited, deliberately letting
misremembering do the composition.

## Themes

Memory, place, erosion. About how shorelines — and stories about
shorelines — wear down with retelling.

## Process

Oil and cold wax on panel. Salt was worked into the wet wax in the
lower third, then brushed away, leaving pitting Zak kept.
