# Wintering Light — artist notes (SAMPLE — replace with Zak's real notes)

## Inspiration

Painted during the shortest days of the year, facing a north window.
Zak kept this one — it hangs in the studio and is lent to the show so
the series reads complete.

## Themes

Light, seasons, keeping. The only work in *Thresholds* about staying
rather than crossing.

## Process

Oil on linen — the only piece in the show not on panel, and the only
one without cold wax. "I wanted one surface in the room that behaved
differently."
