# Threshold No. 3 — artist notes (SAMPLE — replace with Zak's real notes)

## Inspiration

The third in a series of attempts to paint a doorway that "won't stay
still." Zak kept repainting the same interior threshold from the
childhood home, each version less literal than the last.

## Themes

Thresholds, repetition, movement. The series is about returning to the
same image until the image stops being about the place and starts being
about the returning.

## Process

Oil, cold wax, and graphite on panel. Graphite underdrawing is left
visible along the left edge. Zak calls these "the scaffolding I
decided not to remove."
