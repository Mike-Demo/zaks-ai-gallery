# After the Rain — artist notes (SAMPLE — replace with Zak's real notes)

## Inspiration

Painted from memory of a walk home after a summer storm, when the
pavement threw the streetlights back doubled. Zak wanted to hold "wet
pavement light, a moment longer than it lasts."

## Themes

Memory, weather, light. A companion to Blue Passage — both are about
surfaces that remember something briefly and then let it go.

## Process

Oil and cold wax on panel. Thinner application than the rest of the
series; Zak worked wet-into-wet in a single week to keep the surface
open, then sealed it with one cold wax pass.
