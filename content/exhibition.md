# Thresholds

## A solo exhibition by Zak

Six works about the moments between things: the hallway before the
door opens, the season before it turns. Zak paints the passages we move
through rather than the places we arrive.

These paintings were made over eighteen months in Zak's studio,
working in oil and cold wax on panel. Layers are built up and
scraped back; what remains is what survived the crossing.

*Thresholds* runs October 9 – November 21, 2026.
